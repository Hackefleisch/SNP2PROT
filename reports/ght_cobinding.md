# How much of the GHT panel binds the same loci

Generated by `scripts/check_ght_cobinding.py`, over the benchmark's **test**-chromosome peaks only. It exists to say what the `aliens` negative set actually asks, and what the ceiling on a protein-blind shortcut is.

| | median | min | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| alien windows that are a **panel** TF's peak | 0.493 | 0.373 | 0.695 |
| own positives another **panel** TF also binds | 0.379 | 0.078 | 0.933 |

**Half of every TF's alien negatives are a peak of a TF the model trained on.** The model was shown those exact loci as positives — for a different protein. A model that answers *does this window look bound* has to get them wrong; only a model that conditions on the protein can get them right. That makes `aliens` the sharpest control in the set, and the one a per-TF baseline is not even asked.

**And a median 38% of a TF's own positives are bound by another panel TF too.** That is the size of the protein-blind shortcut on the positive side: co-bound promoters and enhancers where the answer is the same for several proteins at once. It is why `protein = constant` is not a strawman control but a genuinely strong model, and why a Setting 1 auPRC means little until it is read against it.
