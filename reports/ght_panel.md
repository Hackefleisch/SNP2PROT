# The GHT-SELEX panel

Generated by `scripts/build_ght_panel.py`. The benchmark ships 139 TFs (`TFs_CHS+AFS.txt`); this is what the admission policy makes of them.

**The panel is decided by `snp2prot.domains`, not by the `DBD(s)` label** (`docs/GHT_PLAN.md` §11). The plan's own §4 counted 47 usable TFs by subtracting the 92 labelled `C2H2 ZF` from 139. That is a count of what the curators believe each protein contains; the policy counts what a Pfam scan finds and whether the found region is solely responsible, contiguous and resolvable. The two disagree, and this table is the disagreement.

| | count |
|---|---:|
| benchmark TFs | 139 |
| admitted | **33** |
| rejected | 106 |

### The `no_domain` rejections are mostly a family-list gap, not an absent domain

11 of the 13 have Pfam hits and none of those hits is on `data/external/pfam/dbd_families.txt`, which was generated from UniPROBE's and CIS-BP's own curation and therefore covers the families **those** corpora contain. The human Codebook panel reaches families they do not. The remaining 2 have no Pfam hit anywhere in the construct at all, above the gathering threshold, and are a genuine absence rather than a coverage gap.
