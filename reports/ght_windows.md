# GHT-SELEX windows

Generated by `scripts/build_ght_windows.py` from the MEX-ArChIPelago release (`10.5281/zenodo.15555251`) and hg38. Positives are the benchmark's own 301 bp summit-centred peaks; the negative sets and the chromosome split are theirs unchanged (`docs/GHT_PLAN.md` D3-D5).

| Train | `random` | 2,147,220 |
| Train | `shades` | 418,519 |

411 windows were dropped for carrying a base outside `ACGT` (0.0045% of the total). The release's `N` exclusion says this should not happen; the count is small enough to be assembly gaps at contig edges and is reported rather than repaired. The per-(TF, split, set) counts are in `data/interim/ght/window_summary.parquet`, column `n_dropped`.

**`random` and `aliens` are subsampled to 10:1**, seeded, because the released sets are 100:1 and 40 million windows is 12 GB of tokens for a control that is read on auROC. auROC is invariant to the imbalance; **auPRC on those two sets is therefore not comparable to the published 100:1 numbers** and is reported against its own chance level like every other per-protein AUPR in this project.
