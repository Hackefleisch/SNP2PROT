## What the merge step did

- **48 of 1386 domain records dropped** as duplicate measurements of one domain: more positives wins, unless one candidate belongs to a variant series (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, 2026-08-18). Every resolution is listed in `overlap.md`.
- Nothing else. No label, threshold or score was touched, no-call rows were kept, and records with no positive evidence were kept and left flagged in `label_health.md` for the training filter to decide.
